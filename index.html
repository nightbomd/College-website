Overview

The University of Southern California (USC) is a private research university located in Los Angeles, California. Its motto is "The Truth Shall Make You Free." and promotes Diversity.

Usc is known for its strong emphasis on the arts and cinematic studies, as well as its diverse student body and commitment to social justice.

"At USC, you have the chance to become exactly who you want to be while supported and surrounded by a community of other ambitious, talented students who are also pursuing their dreams."

USC School of Cinematic Arts

USC's School of Cinematic Arts allows students to study areas such as film production, animation, screenwriting, interactive media, and game design.

Located in Los Angeles, students are surrounded by one of the world's largest film and entertainment industries.

Students can gain hands-on experience creating films, games, animations, and other media while working with students from different cinematic disciplines.

USC Viterbi School of Engineering

USC's school of engineering specifically allows you to combine engineering majors with other USC such as CS/AI, business, arts, cinema, or music.

As of 2026, USC also has a new B.S in Artificial Intelligence.

Engineering courses are available to freshman and so are research opportunities without any prior experience.

USC Marshall School of Business

USC Marshall focuses on business, leadership, entrepreneurship, and global business.

Students can combine business with other fields at USC, including technology, entertainment, real estate, and entrepreneurship.

Its Los Angeles location also gives students access to internships and career opportunities with major companies, startups, and entertainment businesses.

Financial Aid

The University of Souther California Is a Need-Blind school.That means USC does not discriminate against poor applicants.

The USC scholarship consideration deadline is December first. Some scholarships include:

  • The Trustee scholarship, which offers a full ride
  • the Gates scholarship, which offers full tuition
  • the Presidential scholarship, which offers partial tuition
  • the Dean's scholarship, which offers a merit-based award (around quater tuition)

USC's affordability intitative ensures families under $80,000 in income pay no tuition. However, schools like Northwestern and Uchicago provide free tuition for families earning $200,000 with full rides around $150,000.
